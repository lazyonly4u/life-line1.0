Quiz

Warning!!

Quiz that helps users understand whether they might want to learn more or reach out for professional support, it is NOT a diagnostic test.

Depression

In the past 2 weeks, have you…

  • Felt sad, empty, or low most days?
  • Lost interest in activities you normally enjoy?
  • Felt tired or low-energy a lot of the time?
  • Found it hard to focus or make decisions?
  • Felt like everyday tasks were overwhelming?

Result:

If you said “yes” to several questions and these feelings affect your daily life, it may help to talk with a mental-health professional or trusted person.

Anxiety

Do you often…

  • Feel tense, worried, or on edge even without a clear reason?
  • Notice your heart racing, shaking, or shortness of breath?
  • Have moments where fear suddenly spikes?
  • Avoid situations because they make you anxious?
  • Find it hard to relax or calm your mind?

Result:

If many of these feel familiar, you may benefit from exploring anxiety resources or speaking with a professional.

PTSD

In the past month, have you…

  • Had unwanted memories or strong reactions to reminders of a past event?
  • Experienced nightmares or flashbacks?
  • Felt jumpy, alert, or easily startled?
  • Avoided places, memories, or people connected to the event?
  • Had trouble feeling safe or grounded?

Result:

If these experiences feel strong or persistent, you may want to talk with a trauma-informed professional for support.

Eating Disorders (ED)

Do you ever…

  • Feel distressed about food, eating, or your body image?
  • Experience strong urges to restrict, binge, or compensate?
  • Feel guilt, shame, or fear related to eating?
  • Avoid meals or feel out of control with food?
  • Notice eating patterns affecting your daily life?

Result:

If these questions feel relatable and cause distress, reaching out to a professional can be supportive and helpful.

ADHD

Do you often…

  • Struggle to stay focused or remember what you were doing?
  • Get easily distracted or overwhelmed?
  • Feel restless or have trouble sitting still?
  • Act on impulses before thinking them through?
  • Have difficulty finishing tasks or organizing things?

Result:

If you relate to several of these and they’ve been consistent for many years, exploring ADHD resources or talking to a professional might help.

OCD

In the past month, have you...

  • Experienced unwanted thoughts or images that were difficult to ignore?
  • Felt driven to repeat certain actions or mental rituals?
  • Spent a lot of time checking, cleaning, counting, or seeking reassurance?
  • Felt anxious when you tried not to perform these behaviors?
  • Found these thoughts or behaviors interfering with daily life?

Result:

If you relate to several of these and they’ve been consistent for many years, exploring ADHD resources or talking to a professional might help.

Short informal text

Depression

  • A long-lasting low mood or loss of interest that affects daily life.

    • Anxiety

  • Strong worry or fear that activates the body’s stress response.

    • PTSD

  • A trauma-related condition involving flashbacks, anxiety, and distress.

    • Eating Disorders

  • Serious conditions involving difficult thoughts and behaviors about food and body image.

    • ADHD

  • A neurodevelopmental condition involving inattention, hyperactivity, or impulsivity.

    • OCD

  • OCD (Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder) causes unwanted thoughts (obsessions) and repetitive behaviors (compulsions) that can interfere with daily life.
